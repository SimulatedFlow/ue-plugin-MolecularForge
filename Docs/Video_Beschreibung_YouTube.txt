Load real protein structures into Unreal Engine 5.8 and render them five ways — space-filling, ball-and-stick, backbone, cartoon ribbon and Gaussian surface — switchable at runtime on a single actor.

What you see in this video is the mesoscale demo: 780 molecules in two species, spawned as Mass entities, moving by Brownian diffusion, reflecting off the bounds of the volume, and associating and dissociating with per-second rates. The small orange ligands you see sitting on the larger blue proteins are bound, not decorative.

WHAT THE PLUGIN DOES

• Reads PDB and mmCIF directly in the engine — fixed-column and token-based. Element, residue, chain and B-factor are kept for every atom. The same entry in both formats parses to the same atoms, and an automated test checks that atom by atom.
• Fetches structures from the RCSB Protein Data Bank by PDB ID, or from the AlphaFold database by UniProt accession, with an on-disk cache and a background parse that does not stall the game thread.
• Five representations on one actor, switchable at runtime.
• Secondary structure computed in-engine after Kabsch & Sander (1983), or taken from the file's own HELIX/SHEET records.
• A PyMOL-style selection language: chain A and resi 10-25 and not name CA — parsed by recursive descent, usable from Blueprint.
• Measurements: distance, angle and dihedral with the IUPAC sign convention.
• DCD trajectory playback with frame interpolation, both byte orders.
• Mesoscale crowding on Unreal's Mass framework, with distance-staggered drawing.
• Niagara handoff through array parameters — any particle system can read the structure.
• Colour by element (CPK), chain, secondary structure, B-factor, or AlphaFold pLDDT confidence.

HONEST LIMITS

Atoms are drawn as real sphere geometry through instanced static meshes. That is comfortable for structures of a few thousand atoms; it is not a renderer for a whole virus capsid. Trajectory support is DCD only — XTC needs a compression library and is not included. The mesoscale layer models diffusion and binding; it is not a molecular-dynamics force field and does not claim to be one.

REQUIREMENTS

Unreal Engine 5.8, Windows. Depends only on engine plugins that ship with the editor: ProceduralMeshComponent, Niagara and MassGameplay. Five runtime C++ modules, 53 automation tests that run offline, three example levels, three demo structures.

DATA LICENCES

The demo structures (crambin 1CRN, green fluorescent protein 1EMA, human deoxyhaemoglobin 4HHB) come from the RCSB Protein Data Bank. Structures downloaded at runtime carry the licence of their source — AlphaFold models are CC-BY 4.0, and naming the source is a condition of that licence. The plugin supplies the attribution string on every loaded structure.

DISCLOSURE
