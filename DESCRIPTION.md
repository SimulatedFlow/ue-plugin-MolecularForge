# MolecularForge — Protein & Molecule Visualization for Unreal Engine 5

Load a protein from a file, from the RCSB Protein Data Bank or from the AlphaFold database,
and render it — space-filling, ball-and-stick, backbone, cartoon ribbon or Gaussian surface,
switchable at runtime on a single actor.

Science and education projects in Unreal usually stop at the first hurdle: there is no way
to get a real structure into the engine. You export a mesh from PyMOL, lose every atom
identity on the way, and end up with a prop instead of a model. MolecularForge reads the
archive formats directly — fixed-column PDB and token-based mmCIF — keeps element, residue,
chain and B-factor for every atom, and builds the geometry in the engine. What you get is a
structure you can select into, measure, colour by any scheme and animate, not a static mesh.

## KEY FEATURES

- **Five representations**, switchable on one actor: space-filling, ball-and-stick,
  backbone, cartoon ribbon with helix/sheet/coil profiles, and a Gaussian surface built by
  marching tetrahedra.
- **Reads PDB and mmCIF** — the same entry in both formats parses to the same atoms, and an
  automated test checks exactly that, atom by atom.
- **Downloads structures** from RCSB and from the AlphaFold database, with an on-disk cache
  and a background parse that does not stall the game thread. AlphaFold models are
  recognised and carry their CC-BY attribution string with them.
- **Secondary structure** computed in the engine after Kabsch & Sander (1983) — hydrogen
  bonds, n-turns and bridges — or taken from the file's own HELIX/SHEET records.
- **Selection language** in the style of PyMOL: `chain A and resi 10-25 and not name CA`,
  parsed by recursive descent, usable from Blueprint.
- **Measurements**: distance, angle and dihedral with the IUPAC sign convention, verified
  against an independent handedness test.
- **DCD trajectory playback** with frame interpolation — both byte orders, and files with
  fixed atoms are rejected rather than silently mis-read.
- **Mesoscale crowding** on Unreal's Mass framework: molecules as entities, Brownian
  diffusion with reflecting or wrapping bounds, association and dissociation with per-second
  rates, and distance-staggered drawing. The included demo runs 780 molecules in two
  species.
- **Niagara handoff** — atom positions, radii and colours are pushed into Niagara array
  parameters, so any particle effect can read the structure without custom shader work.
- **Colour schemes**: element (CPK), chain, secondary structure, B-factor, and the AlphaFold
  pLDDT confidence palette.
- **C++ and Blueprint**, five runtime modules, no editor-only dependency at runtime.

## WHAT IS IN THE PACKAGE

Three example levels, all generated by scripts that ship with the plugin, so you can rebuild
them after any change:

- **Showcase** — GFP (1EMA), 1771 atoms in CPK colours.
- **Comparison** — Crambin (1CRN) in all five representations side by side.
- **Mesoscale** — a diffusing, binding molecule population on Mass.

Three demo structures are included (Crambin, GFP, human deoxyhaemoglobin), each as `.pdb`
and `.cif`.

## HONEST LIMITS

- Atoms are drawn as real sphere geometry through instanced static meshes. That is
  comfortable for structures of a few thousand atoms; it is not a renderer for a whole
  virus capsid.
- Trajectory support is DCD only. XTC needs a compression library and is not included.
- The mesoscale layer models diffusion and binding. It is not a molecular-dynamics force
  field and does not claim to be one.

## REQUIREMENTS

Unreal Engine 5.8, Windows. Depends on the engine plugins ProceduralMeshComponent, Niagara
and MassGameplay — all shipped with the engine, nothing to install.

Structures you download at runtime carry the licence of their source. See `ATTRIBUTION.md`
in the plugin folder; AlphaFold data are CC-BY 4.0 and naming the source is a condition of
that licence.
